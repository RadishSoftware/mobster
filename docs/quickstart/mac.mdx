The longest wait is Mobster's helper installing on your iPhone, about 2 minutes the first time.

## Before you begin

- A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 13 or later.
- An iPhone and a USB cable that carries data.
- Xcode, signed in to an Apple Account. A free account works. Setup offers to get Xcode if it's missing, and that download can take an hour.
- The macOS your Xcode version needs ([Apple lists it](https://developer.apple.com/support/xcode/)).
- A plan you already pay for, such as Claude Code or Codex, or an Anthropic or OpenAI API key. You pay the provider directly.

[Requirements](/requirements) lists the rest.

## Set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get Mobster and sign in">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Download">
        [Get Mobster for Mac](https://mobster.dev/download), open `Mobster.dmg` and drag Mobster to Applications.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="Homebrew">
        ```sh
        brew install --cask radishsoftware/tap/mobster-app
        ```

        This puts the same `Mobster.app` in Applications.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>

    Open Mobster and sign in with Apple, Google, GitHub or a code sent to your email. The app updates itself from then on. [Your Mobster account](/account)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what you use Mobster for">
    Mobster asks **What will you use Mobster for?**

    - **My app**: check an iOS app you or your coding agent built.
    - **My accounts**: Instagram and TikTok on your iPhones, such as DMs, comments, posts and reports.

    Your answer picks the first tasks Mobster suggests. You can change it in Settings › General › **What you use Mobster for**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your iPhone">
    **Connect your iPhone** is a checklist that updates as you go. When a row has a button, such as **Get Xcode** or **Install**, press it.

    If Xcode isn't signed in to an Apple Account, the first row says so, with **Open Xcode**. Sign in under Xcode › Settings › Accounts, and the row clears when you come back.

    1. Plug in the iPhone, unlock it and tap **Trust**.
    2. Mobster installs its helper on the iPhone, signed with the Apple Account in Xcode.
    3. On the iPhone, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode, turn it on and restart. iOS shows the switch once the install has started.
    4. Choose **Allow** so Mobster can tap and type.

    The screen then names your iPhone and says it is ready.

    <Frame caption="Connect your iPhone, part way through.">
      <img src="/images/mac/setup-light.webp" alt="Setup's iPhone screen: Connect your iPhone, with a checklist of Xcode, the iPhone, Developer Mode, Mobster's helper installing, and Let Mobster tap and type." className="block dark:hidden" />
      <img src="/images/mac/setup-dark.webp" alt="Setup's iPhone screen: Connect your iPhone, with a checklist of Xcode, the iPhone, Developer Mode, Mobster's helper installing, and Let Mobster tap and type." className="hidden dark:block" />
    </Frame>

    <Note>A free Apple Account signs the helper for 7 days at a time. Mobster refreshes it while the iPhone is plugged in and unlocked.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who runs your tasks">
    - **Use your plan**: choose **Claude Code** or **Codex**. If either is signed in on this Mac, Setup uses it. If neither is signed in, Setup lists plans that support a one-time sign-in code, such as Grok. To add Mobster to your agent, see [Add Mobster to your agent](/agents).
    - **Use an API key**: choose **Anthropic** or **OpenAI**, paste the key and choose **Connect**. Mobster tests the key once.

    With an Anthropic key, Setup says **Claude is connected with the key ending a7f2.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your free trial">
    When your iPhone is ready, choose **Start 7 days free**. The trial needs no card. Mobster charges nothing when it ends: you choose a plan then. [The free trial](/account#the-free-trial)

    An account that already has a plan sees **You're all set** instead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Run a first task

Mobster opens with a first task in the task box. Run it, or replace it with a task that only reads, and press Enter:

```text
What iOS version is this iPhone on?
```

Mobster works one step at a time. Each step shows what it tapped, and the answer comes with the screen it was read from.

## Approve a send

Now ask for something that sends:

```text
Text Sam I'm 10 minutes late
```

Mobster stops before it taps Send and shows you the app and the exact text. Choose **Send** to send it or **Don't send** to stop. With no answer in 10 minutes, the task ends without sending.
