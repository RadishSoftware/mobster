## How it works

Mobster runs on your Mac and drives a simulator, or your iPhone over a USB cable. It reads each screen the way VoiceOver does, as buttons and text with names, so it taps **Send** by name. A check that passed once replays with no model call. By default Mobster stops before anything that sends, buys, posts or deletes, and shows you the exact action, as above ([Approvals and safety](/safety)).

Mobster's agent runs on your own key or on a plan you already pay for, such as Claude Code or Codex. No Mobster server sits in between ([Models and keys](/models-and-keys)). It can't get past Face ID or your passcode.

Mobster for Mac is \$12 a month, \$99 a year or \$149 once, for every phone, after a 7-day free trial with no card ([Pricing](/pricing)). Checks on a simulator are free.
