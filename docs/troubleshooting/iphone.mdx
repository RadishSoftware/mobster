Run `mobster doctor` first. Every line with `✗` has its fix under it. Each heading below is the message you see.

## Connecting

<span id="wda-not-answering"></span>

### ✗ WebDriverAgent  not answering at http://127.0.0.1:8100

Mobster's helper (WebDriverAgent) isn't running on the phone, or the USB relay isn't.

1. Open Mobster for Mac, or run `mobster serve --manage-device`: either starts both and restarts them if they stop.
2. In a source checkout, `scripts/usb-wda.sh` does the same by hand: [Device setup](/device-setup#by-hand).
3. For a simulator, run `mobster sim prepare` and pass `--device NAME` from `mobster devices`.

Only checking your own app? You need none of this: run `mobster sim doctor`.

<span id="no-iphone"></span>

### ! USB iPhone  none connected

1. Use a cable that carries data, not one that only charges, or try another port.
2. Unlock the phone, and check that Developer Mode is on (Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode).
3. `idevice_id -l` should print the phone's UDID.

<span id="not-trusted"></span>

### ! USB iPhone  … connected but not trusted

Unlock the phone, tap **Trust** on "Trust This Computer?", and enter the passcode. If you tapped **Don't Trust**, unplug the phone and plug it in again.

<span id="runner-expired"></span>

### ✗ WDA runner  the runner's signature expired

A free Apple Account signs Mobster's helper for 7 days at a time.

1. In Mobster for Mac, choose **Refresh now** in Settings › Phones, or **Refresh helper** in the sidebar. Mobster also refreshes it by itself while the phone is plugged in and unlocked.
2. Without the Mac app, rebuild it with the guided setup (`setup /build`) or rerun your `xcodebuild build-for-testing`.

<span id="not-registered"></span>

### Building WDA fails with "isn't registered in your developer account"

Open Xcode once with the phone connected, so Xcode registers it on your team, or register it in Apple's developer portal. Then build again.

<span id="iproxy-old"></span>

### iproxy is too old to bind 127.0.0.1 only

Run `brew upgrade libimobiledevice libusbmuxd`. Mobster binds the relay to this Mac only, and older `iproxy` builds can't.

<span id="device-busy"></span>

### ! Device lock  another Mobster process is using this phone

One Mobster process controls a phone at a time. Finish or stop the task in Mobster for Mac, in `mobster serve` or in the other terminal, then try again.

## During a task

<span id="locked"></span>

### Your iPhone is locked. Unlock it and try again. No action was taken.

The phone was locked when the task started. Mobster checks before its first model call, so the task cost nothing.

1. Unlock the phone.
2. Run the task again.

<Note>Mobster never enters your passcode and never unlocks the phone. For long or scheduled tasks, keep it unlocked while it's plugged in: Settings › Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock.</Note>

<span id="locked-mid-task"></span>

### Your iPhone locked during the task, so Mobster stopped.

The phone auto-locked mid-task, often during a long wait for your approval. Unlock it, or lengthen Auto-Lock, and run the task again. In Mobster for Mac, **Pick up where it left off** continues from the last step.

<span id="unplugged"></span>

### Your iPhone was unplugged. Plug it back in and try again.

The cable came out during the task. Plug it back in; Mobster's helper starts again by itself.

<span id="not-responding"></span>

### Your iPhone isn't responding. Unlock it, close Control Center or Siri…

Something covers the app. Close it on the phone and try again. Mobster presses Home before each task, but it can't dismiss everything.

<span id="face-id"></span>

### [App] is asking for Face ID. Mobster can't use Face ID…

The app wants Face ID before it shows anything. Open the app on the phone once yourself, then run the task again.

<span id="app-locked"></span>

### [App] is locked on your iPhone. Mobster won't open locked apps…

The app is locked with iOS's Require Face ID. Open it yourself, or remove the lock: touch and hold its icon, then **Don't Require Face ID**.

<span id="app-store-confirm"></span>

### The App Store wants you to confirm on your iPhone.

A download or purchase needs the side button pressed twice. Mobster never confirms one for you: do it on the phone if you meant it.

`mobster devices lock-probe` shows what Mobster sees on the lock screen and taps nothing.
