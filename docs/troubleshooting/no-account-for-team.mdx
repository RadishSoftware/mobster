You see this: `xcodebuild` prints `error: No Account for Team "<your team id>". Add a new account in Accounts settings or verify that your accounts have valid credentials.` Mobster shows `Sign in again in Xcode › Settings › Accounts`.

## Why it happens

The team is chosen, but Xcode's session for it expired. It is the likeliest way a helper refresh fails days after it worked.

## Fix

1. Open **Xcode › Settings › Accounts**.
2. Sign in to your Apple Account again and select the team.
3. Choose **Check again** in Mobster for Mac, or build again.

## Check it worked

The build finishes and `mobster doctor` prints no `✗` line for the runner.

Checked 26 Oct 2026 against Mobster 0.4.0. Back to [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#iphone).
