You see this: `xcodebuild` prints `error: Signing for "WebDriverAgentRunner" requires a development team. Select a development team in the Signing & Capabilities editor.` Mobster shows `Choose your Apple Account`.

## Why it happens

The helper runs on your iPhone, and iOS only runs apps signed by a team. No team was chosen, or Xcode has no signing certificate for it.

## Fix

1. Sign in to Xcode with your Apple ID: **Xcode › Settings › Accounts**.
2. In Mobster for Mac's setup, choose your Apple Account (your team).
3. If Xcode has no certificate yet, in Accounts choose **Manage Certificates › Apple Development**.
4. Build again.

## Check it worked

The build finishes and `mobster doctor` prints no `✗` line for the runner.

Checked 26 Oct 2026 against Mobster 0.4.0. Back to [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#iphone).
