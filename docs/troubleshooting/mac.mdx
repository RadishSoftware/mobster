**Help** in the sidebar answers questions about setup, your AI account, approvals and the license, offline. For anything else, write to [support@radish.software](mailto:support@radish.software).

## License

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| That isn't a Mobster license key. | Keys look like `MOB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`. Copy it again from the page that opened after checkout |
| We couldn't find that license. | Check the key, or get it again at [mobster.dev/recover](https://mobster.dev/recover) with the email you paid with and the receipt number from Stripe |
| This license is on 3 Macs. That's its limit. | Deactivate one in Settings › License on that Mac, then activate here |
| This Mac was deactivated | Another Mac freed this one's seat. **Activate this Mac** to use Mobster here again |
| Mobster couldn't reach mobster.dev to activate your license. | Check your connection and try again |
| Your last payment didn't go through. | Update your card at [mobster.dev/manage](https://mobster.dev/manage), or in Settings › License › **Manage subscription** |

## Setup

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Xcode is installed but Setup waits | Open Xcode once so it can finish installing. Mobster checks by itself |
| Waiting for your iPhone | Plug it in with a cable that carries data, and unlock it. Try another cable or port: some cables only charge |
| Waiting for Trust | Unlock your iPhone and tap **Trust**, then enter your passcode |
| Developer Mode isn't in Settings | Continue: it appears after Mobster installs its helper, and Setup asks again then |
| Sign in to Xcode first | In Xcode › Settings › Accounts, choose **+** and sign in with your Apple Account |
| The install didn't finish. | Read the fix shown above it, then **Try again**. **Show details** has the log |
| Mobster's helper didn't start | On your iPhone, open Settings › General › VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple Account under Developer App, then tap **Trust** |

## The weekly refresh

A free Apple Account signs Mobster's helper for 7 days at a time. Mobster refreshes it by itself while your iPhone is plugged in and unlocked, and notifies you a day before it needs you.

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| [Name] needs a quick refresh | Choose **Refresh now**. It's quicker than the first install |
| Plug in your iPhone to refresh | Plug it in and unlock it |
| Mobster couldn't refresh its helper | Read the fix under it, then **Refresh now** in Settings › Phones |

## Voice

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Voice needs macOS 26 or later | Update macOS, or type the task |
| Mobster can't hear you | Allow Mobster in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone |
| Speech recognition is off | Allow Mobster in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Speech Recognition |
| Voice stopped working | Restart Mobster |

## Your AI account

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Claude didn't accept this key. | Copy it again from the Claude Console. It starts with `sk-ant-` |
| Your Claude account has no credit yet. | Add credit in the console, then **I've added it** |
| This key can't use the model Mobster's agent runs on yet. | The line under it says where to fix it, then **Test again** |
| Mobster couldn't reach Claude. | Check your internet connection, then try again |

The same messages name OpenAI when that's your account.

## Still stuck

Quit Mobster (⌘Q) and open it again. Setup's logs are in `~/Library/Application Support/app.mobster.desktop/logs`. Read them before you send them: they can hold what was on your screen.
