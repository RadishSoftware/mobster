You see this: `xcodebuild` prints `error: unable to open output file ... 'No space left on device'` while it compiles the helper.

## Why it happens

The build writes to Mobster's `wda-build` folder in `~/Library/Application Support/app.mobster.desktop/`. Xcode stops when the disk has no room.

## Fix

1. Free up space on your Mac.
2. Build again from Mobster for Mac.

## Check it worked

`mobster sim doctor` prints a `Free space` line with the room left.

Checked 26 Oct 2026 against Mobster 0.4.0. Back to [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#iphone).
