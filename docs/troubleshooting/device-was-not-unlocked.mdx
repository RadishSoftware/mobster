You see this: `xcodebuild` prints this (`com.apple.dt.deviceprep` code -3) when the phone is locked while Mobster starts its helper. Mobster shows `Unlock your iPhone. Mobster's helper starts as soon as it is unlocked.`

## Why it happens

iOS won't start UI automation on a locked phone. Mobster never enters your passcode and never unlocks the phone.

## Fix

1. Unlock the iPhone and keep it unlocked.
2. Wait. Mobster builds again by itself once the phone reads unlocked, and the helper starts about 2 seconds later.
3. For a phone that locks during unattended tasks, lengthen **Settings › Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock**.

## Check it worked

`mobster doctor` prints `✓ WebDriverAgent`. See also [the helper waits for unlock](/troubleshooting#helper-waits-for-unlock).

Checked 26 Oct 2026 against Mobster 0.4.0. Back to [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#iphone).
