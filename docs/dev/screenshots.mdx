`mobster asc screenshots` makes your App Store screenshots from the checks you already have. Each check opens a screen the way your users reach it, by deep link or by its steps. Mobster runs it on a simulator of each App Store display size, in each language your app ships, with the status bar at 9:41, full battery and full bars. Then it saves that screen as a full-resolution PNG.

Nothing is uploaded. The folder is yours to review, then `fastlane deliver` or App Store Connect takes it.

## Make them

Tag the checks that end on a screen you want in the store with `screenshot`, then from your project's folder:

```sh
mobster asc screenshots --size 6.9 --locale en-US --locale de-DE
```

```text
2 screenshots: 1 screen × 6.9" × en-US, de-DE
[1/2] Home greets a returning user: iPhone 17 Pro Max, en-US
[2/2] Home greets a returning user: iPhone 17 Pro Max, de-DE
✓ en-US/01_69_home-greets-a-returning-user.png  1320×2868  (passed)
✓ de-DE/01_69_home-greets-a-returning-user.png  1320×2868  (passed)

Screenshots in fastlane/screenshots, listed in screenshots.json. Review them, then upload with `fastlane deliver` or in App Store Connect. Mobster uploads nothing.
```

That run took 1 min 55 s on an M5 Max under heavy load (14 Oct 2026), most of it creating and booting the iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator the first time.

| Flag | Default |
| --- | --- |
| `PATH` | The checks tagged `screenshot`, else every check in `.mobster/checks`. Each check is one screenshot, numbered in order. |
| `--size` | `6.9`, plus `13` when the app runs on iPad (`UIDeviceFamily`). Also `6.5` and `6.3`. Repeatable. |
| `--locale` | Every language the built app has (its `.lproj` folders), as App Store locales: `en` is `en-US`, `de` is `de-DE`. Repeatable. |
| `-o DIR` | `fastlane/screenshots`, in `<locale>/<NN>_<size>_<check>.png`, the layout `fastlane deliver` reads. |

## How each one is made

- **The language.** The app launches with `-AppleLanguages (de-DE) -AppleLocale de_DE`. iOS stays in its own language. Your app shows the one you asked for.
- **The size.** Each size runs on the newest simulator of it this Mac has: iPhone 17 Pro Max or 16 Pro Max for 6.9", iPad Pro 13-inch for 13". Each PNG's pixel size is checked against App Store Connect's sizes: 1320 × 2868 for 6.9", 2064 × 2752 for 13".
- **The status bar.** `xcrun simctl status_bar … override` sets 9:41, Wi-Fi, full bars and a charged battery for the run, and clears it after.
- **The verdict.** The check's expectations are still checked. A shot whose run couldn't run is left out. `screenshots.json` lists every shot with its verdict, so a German screen that fails an English expectation shows up there.

Checks that open their screen by deep link (`launch.url`) need no model and suit every language. A check with steps replays its recorded steps, which find controls by their labels. In another language those labels differ, so Smart heals the step on your key.
