Mobster's agent runs on your Claude or OpenAI key, billed by your provider: [Models and keys](/models-and-keys). Prices are in US dollars.

## Your license

- **Activate.** On first launch, buy a license in the app, or choose **I have a license key** and paste the key that starts with `MOB-`. It's on the mobster.dev page that opened after checkout.
- **Up to 3 Macs.** A license activates on up to 3 Macs. To move it, deactivate a Mac in Settings › License, then activate the new one.
- **The license check.** Mobster for Mac checks the license with mobster.dev when you activate or deactivate a Mac, and at most once a day on a monthly plan. It sends your key and a hashed id for the Mac, never a screen, a task or anything from the phone. A lifetime license works offline after activation.
- **Lost your key?** Get it again at [mobster.dev/recover](https://mobster.dev/recover) with the email you paid with.

## Refunds and cancelling

- **Lifetime.** Full refund within 14 days if Mobster isn't for you. Write to [support@radish.software](mailto:support@radish.software).
- **Monthly.** Cancel anytime at [mobster.dev/manage](https://mobster.dev/manage), or in Settings › License › **Manage subscription**, which opens the same page. Sign in with the email you paid with to cancel, change your card or download invoices. You aren't charged again, and the license keeps working to the end of the period you paid for.

Questions about a license, a refund or moving to another Mac: [support@radish.software](mailto:support@radish.software). A person reads every message.
