Mobster for Mac is one window: a sidebar on the left, the task page or a conversation in the middle, and your iPhone's screen on the right. The app has its own 1-minute tour too: Help › **Take the tour**.

1. **New task** (⌘N) starts a new conversation.
2. **History**, **Workflows**, **Apps** and **Settings**. ⌘1 to ⌘4 go to the task page, History, Workflows and Apps, and ⌘, opens Settings.
3. **Recent** lists your conversations, newest first. A dot says one is running, waiting for you, or worth a look.
4. **Search** (⌘K) finds conversations, tasks and apps.
5. **The task box.** Type a task. **+** attaches files, **Any app** picks the app it starts in, and **⋯** chooses the answer's format.
6. **Starters** fill the task box with a task to try.
7. **Help** answers questions about setup, your AI account, approvals and the license, offline.

## Conversations

Each task starts a conversation. Mobster shows each step as it goes, and the result as an answer with the screen it came from. Follow up in the same box, and the next task knows what the last one found. [Conversations](/conversations) has the details.

## Approvals and questions

Before anything that sends, buys, posts or deletes, a card shows the exact action and text, with the act's own verb. When a task could mean two things, Mobster may ask you which one, with each choice as a button. [Approvals and safety](/safety) has the rules.

## The phone pane

On the right, your iPhone's screen, live, while Mobster can see it. The phone's name at the top picks another phone when you have several, the hand button lets you tap and type on the phone yourself, and the arrows give the screen more room. When the phone needs something, such as a refresh of Mobster's helper or an unlock, the pane says what to do and offers the one button that does it.

## Search

⌘K opens search over your conversations, tasks and apps. ↑ and ↓ move, Enter opens, Esc closes.

## History and workflows

**History** lists every task you gave your iPhone, what it found and how it ended, with filters for the ones that need a look. **Workflows** holds saved tasks: run one again with a click, or on a schedule. [Schedules and workflows](/schedules) has the details.

## Share a task

**Save as GIF**, under a finished task, saves it to Downloads as a captioned GIF you can post: the task as you typed it, each step's screen with what Mobster did, the approval if it asked, and the result. It matches the app's appearance, light or dark, and stays under X's 15 MB limit. The GIF shows every screen of the task and stays on your Mac until you share it, so look at it first. Screens after a sign-in code are left out. To blur text fields and messages, or to save an MP4, use [`mobster export`](/cli#mobster-export) with `--redact` or `--mp4`.

## Settings

Settings holds your AI account and spend limits, Safety, Memory, Voice, Phones, your license, Help and support, Advanced, and For developers. [Settings](/mac/settings) goes through each one.
