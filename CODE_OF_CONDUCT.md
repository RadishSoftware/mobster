# Code of Conduct

Mobster CLI follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page is a summary. The Covenant's text is the rule.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient with people, including people new to the project.
- Respect different opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and take constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community as a whole.

## What we do not accept

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission. This includes screenshots or journals from someone else's phone.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/RadishSoftware/mobster/security/advisories/new), and start the title with "Conduct". Reports are read by the maintainers only, handled promptly and fairly, and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on the impact of the behavior.
