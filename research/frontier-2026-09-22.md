# Mobile agent frontier: literature scan (2026-09-22)

Literature scan by a research sub-agent, with sources. Items marked
[unverified] are self-reported or come from an aggregator. Treat every number
as a pointer to check, not a settled fact.

## Headlines
- Easy benchmarks are saturated and now measure noise. AndroidWorld: 91–99%
  claimed, against an 80% human baseline; the leaderboard is unverified.
  OSWorld-Verified is around 85% [aggregator]. Label and evaluator error runs
  5–15% or more (AndroidControl: more than 70% of samples defective,
  arXiv 2510.18488).
- Realistic benchmarks cap today at roughly 40–75%: MobileWorld 52–76%
  (arXiv 2512.19432), AndroidDaily 62% at 14.7 s/step (arXiv 2605.27761),
  iOSWorld 52% (arXiv 2606.09764, simulator only), VenusBench 37% with at most
  15% stability (arXiv 2604.06182), OSWorld 2.0 21% (arXiv 2606.29537).
- iOSWorld: adding the accessibility tree is worth +18–26 points for frontier
  models. No benchmark on real iOS devices exists.
- Verification is a first-class bottleneck. False success accounts for 45–79%
  of failures. LLM judges reach AUROC 0.54–0.65 at detecting it; TF-IDF plus
  XGBoost detectors reach 0.83–0.95 (arXiv 2606.09863).
- Latency: 76–96% is LLM time (arXiv 2506.16042). Published agents run 3–40
  s/step; ios-use's XCTest driver measured a tree dump at 241 ms against 966 ms
  for WDA (github.com/xhzq233/ios-use).

## Techniques that need no RL on the policy
- Conformal or selective action, with a guaranteed error rate: SafeGround
  (2602.02419), CORA (2604.09155).
- Cheap information probes and rollback: MobileExplorer (2605.26546).
- World-model lookahead: MobileDreamer (2601.04035), +5.3 points at 1.9× latency.
- Candidate-scoring verifiers: V-Droid (2503.15937). Weak-verifier ensembles:
  Weaver (2506.18203). Comparative judging: Behavior Best-of-N (2510.02250).
- Replay with preconditions, parameter slots and tiered fallback: EchoPath
  (2609.16635), 87–93% replay success. MAS-Bench (2509.06477): naive
  task-level replay succeeds 10% of the time; shortcuts and deep links 100%.
- Speculative actions (2510.04371) and speculative macro commit (2609.03236).
- Hidden accessibility nodes are an injection vector: "Not an A11y"
  (2608.08939). Defences are visibility filtering, provenance tags, and
  treating accessibility text as data.

## Open problems (no published solution found)
1. Calibrated verification of multi-hop extractive answers.
2. Safe backtracking on stateful real devices.
3. Replay that survives layout drift after app updates.
4. Canvas-drawn state without a vision model every step.
5. A benchmark on real iOS devices.

Full suite, 14 tasks × 3 on the USB iPhone: **39/42 (r3), 38/42 (r4)**, up
from 30/42 before these changes. Remaining failures: web search on Google
result pages (1–2/3), an occasional wrong link on follow-link, and one grader
reset.

Not yet built: conformal action sets (they need per-decision correctness
labels, which the suite does not produce), speculative next-action drafting,
visibility cross-checks for hidden layers (Chrome), and a sparse frontier
planner.
